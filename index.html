Elemental Serenity

An immersive 3D nature experience built with cutting-edge web technologies. Explore a dynamic world that changes with seasons and time, featuring realistic grass simulation, particle effects, and atmospheric lighting.

Technology Stack

Three.js
WebGL
GLSL Shaders
GSAP
Particle Systems
Procedural Generation

Features

  • Dynamic day/night cycle with realistic lighting
  • Seasonal changes affecting environment and atmosphere
  • Advanced grass rendering with wind simulation
  • Realistic fire and smoke particle effects
  • Procedural terrain generation
  • Immersive audio design
  • Optimized performance across devices
